A Data Story

Bangladesh's Shifting Hazard Map

26 Years of Climate Exposure

District-level cyclone, flood, drought, river-level, and coastal-erosion data from 2000–2025.

Research Question

Across cyclone exposure, flood impact, drought severity, river water level, and coastal erosion, which districts in Bangladesh show the most persistent compound climate-hazard vulnerability between 2000 and 2025, which individual hazard is the strongest driver nationally, and is there a measurable relationship between river water level and flood impact severity?

A note on limitations: Figures describe recorded hazard indicators, not damage or casualties. Missing district-year records are shown as "No data," never estimated. All relationships described are associative, not causal.
